Antwerp Brilliant Games

November 2026

Get ready for an unforgettable sports weekend in and around the fabulous city of Antwerp!

Active Company Antwerp proudly presents the Antwerp Brilliant Games, a vibrant multi-sport tournament celebrating community, fun sports, and the joy of competition. Mark your calendars for the weekend of November 6-8, 2026 and prepare to shine in one of our three participating sports disciplines: Badminton, Tennis or Volleyball.

Registrations will open on July 21st, 2026 10 A.M. CEST.

Overview:

  • Meet & Greet (incl. welcome drink): 6 Nov, 2026
  • Badminton & Volleyball tournament: 7 Nov, 2026
  • Tennis tournament: 6-8 Nov, 2026
  • Closing party (at Woodpop): 7 Nov, 2026

More information per tournament can be found on the seperate web pages on this website.
